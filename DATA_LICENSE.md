# Map data and licensing

The country outline, state/region polygons, and major-river centerlines in
`src/data/mapData.ts` were extracted from the Natural Earth vector repository:

- `ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson`
- `ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson`
- `ne_10m_rivers_lake_centerlines.geojson`

Natural Earth states that its raster and vector data are in the public domain
and that no permission is required. The atlas nevertheless credits the source
in its interface and documentation.

- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Vector repository: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector

The resource symbols, terrain treatment, information-panel design, and all
other visual elements in this repository are original project assets.

Resource markers are approximate explanatory anchors. They must not be reused
as precise mine, concession, well, platform, or territorial-control locations.
